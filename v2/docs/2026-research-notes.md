# 2026 Tool-Market Research Notes (Traffic × Earning × CTR × Growth)

> Supporting research for `2026-tool-opportunities.md` and `competitor-tools-catalog.md`.
> Compiled Aug 2026 from the sources linked below.

---

## 🚦 High Traffic (search volume signals)

- **Word counter** = most-searched text utility **~2.4M/month** (VirtualTextTools 2026).
- **"Free online text tools"** searches grew **+340% over 5 years** (Google Trends via VirtualTextTools 2026).
- **File converters** dominate web-tool niches: iLovePDF/iLoveIMG, CloudConvert (212 formats), Convertio, Zamzar (1,200+ formats) are consistent top-traffic web properties.
- **Pi7 largest clusters** (from site nav): exact-KB compression, passport/ID sizes, DPI conversion, background removal → these are the underserved long-tail.
- **High-volume standalone queries confirmed in 2026:** passport photo maker (500K+), image to text / OCR (400K+), MP4 to MP3 (900K+), word/excel to pdf (900K+), video compressor (300K+), pdf to word (800K+).

## 💰 High Earning (CPC / affiliate / RPM)

Top-2026 AdSense CPC niches (SiteWorthIt, Adstimate, MegaDigital, Adit's):
- Insurance $40–80, Personal-injury law ~$90, Online degrees ~$113, SaaS/AI software $10–$117, finance/banking $35+.
- **Tool-site lesson:** tools pulling *commercial/buyer-intent* traffic (finance, business naming, invoicing, professional documents) earn exponentially more per visitor than entertainment utilities.
- **Passport/ID photo tools** are pre-purchase (print sheets, e-sign, visa) → very high RPM; competitors (PhotoAiD, Passport Photo Online) charge $6.99–$16.95.
- **Best 2026 affiliate niche:** AI & SaaS (MonetizeMore) — recurring 20–40% commissions, lifetime value.

## 🖱️ High CTR (intent strength)

- **Developer tools** have the *highest CTR* — exact-intent searches (favicon, base64, regex, JSON) convert clicks at top rates (plugin's own insight #6).
- **Official/exact-size niches** (passport 2x2, 3.5x4.5, PAN card, reduce to 100KB) have sharp query→result match → strong CTR.
- **Comparison/roundup intent** (all the "best free X 2026" articles) shows the demand breadth behind each tool type.

## 📈 Fastest Growth 2026

- **AI & SaaS** = fastest-growing category (Cursor, Perplexity, ElevenLabs, HeyGen breakout) — theaiselect.
- **Next-gen image formats** (WebP, AVIF, JPEG-XL) — TinyPNG added AVIF/JXL; Chrome/Firefox restored JXL in 2026.
- **Privacy-first browser tools** (no-upload, WebAssembly) — the #1 2026 trust/ranking lever (VirtualTextTools, ImageCompressor, SqueezeVid all lead with it).
- **AI-adjacent doc tools:** OCR, AI summarizer, translate, PDF→Markdown (iLovePDF "Intelligence" line added 2026).
- **Security/password:** NIST SP 800-63B (2026) raised min to 15 chars → passphrase generators growing.
