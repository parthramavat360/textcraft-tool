## KEY INSIGHTS FROM RESEARCH

1. Font/Text style generators are the #1 traffic driver for text tool sites. ConvertCase gets millions of visits from these alone. **DONE.**

2. Social media font generators (TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, Discord) each have their own search volume - separate pages = separate SEO entries = more total traffic. **DONE.**

3. Image tools (crop, rotate, watermark) are extremely high volume. **DONE.**

4. Color tools (picker, converter, palette) are surprisingly high volume and very easy to build client-side. **Picker and Converter DONE.**

5. Calculator tools (BMI, age, percentage, tip) are evergreen and high-volume. **DONE.**

6. Developer tools have moderate volume but VERY high CTR - devs search with specific intent and convert well. **DONE.**

7. AI tools are the fastest-growing category in 2026. AI detector and text humanizer are the hottest tools right now. **PENDING.**

8. Cheat sheets and reference pages are easy wins for SEO - they attract links and have long-tail traffic. **3 of 6 DONE.**

9. Build ONE mega-widget for related tools (e.g., one Font Generator with 25 styles) rather than 25 separate widgets. Then create separate WordPress pages for each style/platform targeting different keywords. **DONE.**

10. The biggest ROI is in tools that are easy to build (client-side, no API) but have massive search volume. Font generators, case converters, and simple calculators fit this perfectly. **DONE.**
